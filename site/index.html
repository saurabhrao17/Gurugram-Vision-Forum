      About the Forum

      A citizens' platform that gets Gurugram's problems to the right desk and follows them to closure.

      How it is run

      Advisory board8 to 12 experts from corporate, legal, academic, planning and media backgrounds.
      City executive committeeRuns operations, government liaison and policy work.
      Area chapters, volunteers, youth fellowsSector and ward teams, sewa drives, research support.

                              Privacy notice
                              
    
                              What we collect. When you report an issue we store the issue, the place, your description, your name and mobile number and, if you give them, your email and the documents you attach. The Forum uses them to map the report, file it with the responsible desk on your behalf when you ask, and tell you what happened.
                              
    
                              What is public. No report is ever published on its own. The dashboard and the ward table show counts only: how many reports there are by issue type, ward and stage. Your name, mobile number, email, description and documents are never shown publicly and are sent to no one except the official desk the report is filed with.
                              
    
                              Who sees your details. Only the Forum's signed-in volunteers for your ward and its coordinators, each of whom agrees in writing to use them for the report alone.
                              
    
                              Messages. We message you only about your report, on the channel you used.
                              
    
                              Retention. Reports are deleted 24 months after they close, with their attachments. To limit abuse we keep a salted hash of your connection address for one hour, never the address itself.
                              
    
                              Your choices. To see, correct or delete what we hold, send a request through the Join form: write "Data request" and your reference number in the note. The team acts within 30 days. Join the Forum

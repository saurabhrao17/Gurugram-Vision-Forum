| 8 Oct 2026 | Missing items are asked for from the ticket itself, by ticket ID, and the reply comes back onto the ticket | Owner's ask: the earlier button opened the volunteer's own mail app. Now the desk mails the reporter from the Forum's address with the reference in the subject and Reply-To the desk; the inbound webhook links any mail carrying a reference to its report, stores its photos on it and logs the reply; the volunteer reads the reply on the ticket and lets the AI chain propose values for the missing fields, applied with one click. | 8 Oct 2026 | Content and Newsletter are publishing workspaces; Roles split into Team and Ward assignments | Owner's ask: the pages read as plain forms, and a growing team needs people and ward cover handled separately.
